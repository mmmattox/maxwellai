# Sample teardown — on-device PDF tools (Chrome)
**Purpose:** Deliverable-quality sample (not a live client job).  
**Date:** 2026-09-13  
**Method:** Public pages only. On-page numbers cited; else **BLIND**.

## Job to be done
People already search for PDF merge / split / compress. Incumbents train the market to **upload**. Wedge: same jobs, file never leaves the device, honest free cap, paid unlock without a call.

## Competitors (see CSV)
### Smallpdf (CWS)
- Listing title emphasizes edit/convert/compress + AI summarize; copy: “Pick a tool and **upload** your file”; “Get Smallpdf Pro.”
- Store signal (on page 2026-09-13 fetch): **4.6 / 3K ratings**; version 0.23.21; updated **Jul 16, 2026**. User count on this fetch: not clearly isolated → treat “1B trusted” as marketing, not CWS users.
- Pro angle on listing: unlimited usage, strong compression, OCR conversion, batch — i.e. paywall after free limits.

### iLovePDF (official pricing, fetched 2026-09-13)
- Free: essential tools, limited processing.
- Premium: **$5/mo billed annually ($60/yr)** or **$9/mo** monthly — full tools, unlimited processing, web/mobile/desktop, signatures, workflows, ad-free, priority support, AI credits.
- Trustpilot **4.9** and “16.5M docs/day” are site marketing claims (not independent audit).

### Clone swarm
Dozens of low-rated “free PDF” CWS listings. Race-to-bottom on price/features; lose on privacy honesty + cancel UX.

## Wedge that sells without a call
1. Title matches search intent + “on your device / never uploaded.”
2. Free: **3 ops/day**; Paid: unlimited + batch — market already trained by Smallpdf/iLovePDF caps.
3. Price wedge: **$29 one-time** or **$4/mo** under Premium cloud.
4. Disclose paid features **before** install (CWS policy).
5. Never claim HIPAA/SOC2/“zero telemetry” without evidence.

## Do not build in v1
OCR · PDF↔Office · e-sign · password cracking · AI chat — support + policy landmines.

## Next build step (for a buyer in this niche)
MV3 side panel + `pdf-lib` local ops + off-store license key (Lemon Squeezy/Stripe). Listing before ads. Cap file size honestly (e.g. 50MB).
